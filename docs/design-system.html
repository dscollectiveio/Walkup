Walkup design package

Approved 2026-10-19. The written rules are in docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md. Values are taken from the app's own tokens (globals.css) and shared styles, so this describes the style the app already has. If the app and this page disagree, one of them is wrong and gets fixed.

1. Principles

  • Plain English first, number second. A sentence says what is true ("You're all paid up"), the figure supports it. The reader is a volunteer board member doing this in the evening.
  • Calm and warm. Cream ground, white cards, navy structure, gold as the single accent. No shadows on cards, no gradients, no decoration that carries no meaning.
  • Show your work. Every number can be traced. Status is never carried by color alone: it always has words.
  • Never clever with money. Walkup prepares and reminds; the board decides. Copy and controls must not imply Walkup moves money or sends email unapproved.
  • Everyone can use it. Contrast of 4.5:1 or better, a visible gold focus ring on everything, 44px touch targets on forms, motion that respects "reduce motion".

Rules for gold

5. Headers (four levels)

The rule: the page-level card gets the strong navy band; anything inside a card gets a soft tint with a gold edge.

1. Page card header

Navy band, cream or white eyebrow title, muted hint. Buttons on it are light. Keeps its navy in dark mode (a slightly bluer navy).
Used for every top-level card: Where every unit stands, Transactions, Units and people, Plan and billing.
2. Section header
Soft navy tint (7%), gold left edge, 12px bold caps. For sections inside a card: The unit, People in this unit, Association, Location, Building, Insurance.
3. Summary card header
Same bar, smaller (11px). For small summary cards: Building details, Insurance policy, Owner.
4. Item header (stronger tint, 14%)
For a header that carries content and controls: each unit on the Dues tab (name, owner, standing, owed, plan, Expand).

6. Buttons

Variants

Primary is navy with white text (cream in dark mode). One primary per area. Secondary is white with a strong border. Danger is white with a brick border and text, used for the confirm step of a delete.

Sizes

44px for forms and anything a thumb must hit. 32px and 28px only inside dense cards such as unit rows, where the surrounding row is itself clickable.

On a navy band

3 deposits to match

Light button, navy text. A status pill can sit beside it.

Panel toggles (open one at a time)

Navy buttons; the open one gets a gold ring. Opening one closes the others.

Expand / Collapse

Destructive actions: two steps, inline

Delete → Deletes the record as if it never happened.Cancel

Delete starts as a small red text link. It turns into a plain-words warning plus a Confirm button, with Cancel beside it. Never a pop-up.

  • Do: verbs the person would say (Record a payment, Take them off this unit).
  • Do: keep labels sentence case and short.
  • Don't: make two navy buttons compete for attention in one area.
  • Don't: show a bare "Submit". Say what happens.

7. Forms and inputs

Dollar boxes format as you type: $5,400. Cents allowed ($1,234.50). The form saves the plain number.
Whole numbers with commas.
One decimal always shown: 1.0, 2.0, 2.5. Settles to the nearest half.
USPS confirms this address.
Suggestions as you type; a Unit / Apt box beside it; always overridable.

Choice controls

OwnerProperty manager

Two to four options that switch a mode: a pill-shaped segmented control, selected = navy. Radios and checkboxes use a full 44px row with a border that darkens when chosen.

Rules

  • Label above the box, 13px medium. Help text 12px mute below it. Errors 13px in the bad color, next to the box, and focus moves to the first problem.
  • Optional fields say "optional" in soft text. Required ones are simply labeled.
  • Inputs have a 1px strong border, 8px radius, white fill.
  • Money and numbers are right-aligned inside boxes and use tabular figures.

One per row, full width. Strong-tint header with gold edge, a condensed progress strip when closed. Opened, it shows owed and next charge on the left, panel buttons on the right, then the bars at the bottom.

Accordion

When a list of items can be opened (units on Building & Owners), only one is open at a time; opening another closes the first. The open item is also lit on the building graphic with a soft gold wash.

Two-step save and cancel

Edit opens the form in place (the card grows taller). Save is navy, Cancel is plain text. Nothing saves until Save.

Where to find things

Setting up and changing live on a tab's own page; Your account (the gold circle, top right) holds profile, plan, security and data. Walkup's own billing is never in the sidebar.

11. Motion

  • Color and border changes 300ms. Bars grow over 500ms. The sidebar slides in 160ms. Landing sections fade and rise over 800ms as you scroll.
  • Animation helps you see what changed, never decorates. Everything respects the "reduce motion" setting (it drops to none).

12. Dark mode

  • Cream and navy swap roles. Page = deep navy, cards = navy, text = cream. Navy bands stay navy (a lighter, bluer navy) so headers still read as headers.
  • Status colors lighten until they pass contrast; every pair is documented in docs/reference/signal-theme.md.
  • Rule: never write a color value in a component; use a token and both themes work.

13. Copy and content

  • No em dashes in anything a person reads. Use a period, comma or colon.
  • Plain words, short sentences, say what happens next. "Delete is for a record entered by mistake" beats "Records will be purged."
  • Say who: "a board admin can…", "only the board sees…".
  • Walkup never says it moves, holds or sends money. Email is "sent only after a board admin approves it"; everything else is a draft in your own mail app.
  • Money: dollars with a sign and commas ($5,400), cents only when they matter. Dates written out (June 1, 2026) in Chicago time.
  • Names: the product is Walkup. Tabs: Dashboard, Announcements, Bank Feed & Transactions, Financial Statements, Budget & Spending, HOA Dues, Taxes, Insurance, Building & Owners, Repairs & Upkeep, Find a Contractor, Documents.

14. Accessibility checklist

  • Visible 2px gold focus ring everywhere, never removed.
  • Contrast 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and component edges.
  • Touch targets 44px on forms and primary actions.
  • Every control has a label or aria-label; toggles use aria-expanded; segmented controls use radiogroup; status text is also readable by screen readers.
  • Nothing important by color alone: standing is a word, a quarter is a number.

15. Marketing site (the exceptions)

  • Landing page bands rotate: taupe hero, then blue, taupe, white down the page. Blue bands re-map the tokens so cards and text just work.
  • Yellow (gold) buttons have blue (navy) text. The header is sticky with a dividing line. Sections fade in on scroll.
  • Same typeface, same tokens, same plain voice as the app.

Decisions recorded

  1. Two header strengths: sections 7%, items 14%.
  2. Two button heights: 44px on forms, 28 to 32px inside dense cards.
  3. Expand / Collapse buttons are navy; Your account keeps its pill tab strip.
  4. Buttons on a navy band are light with navy text.
  5. Gold is the only accent; paid-progress bars are green.
  6. Older pages are brought in line with this document.
